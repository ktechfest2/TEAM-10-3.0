| 27 | 19 Nov | **Accounts and log-in: username + a password the person chooses** (6 or more characters, any characters, no forced symbols). Stored as scrypt with a salt. A new phone logs in and gets its **own key on the same account** (`actors.account_id`), never a copy of the old one. "My other phone is lost" signs out every other key (`revoked_at`): a revoked key can no longer author or witness anything new, its earlier records stay valid. (A first version used a one-time recovery code; replaced because people will not keep a 16-character code.
